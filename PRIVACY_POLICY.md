# PRIVACY POLICY FOR SWITCHGATE INTERNET

**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

SwitchGate Internet ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by **Technical amit (education)**, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use SwitchGate Internet on Windows devices.

---

### 1. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USAGE

SwitchGate Internet is designed as a **privacy-first, local-only network management tool**. 

#### Information Processed Locally:
To provide core functionalities (such as local device discovery, bandwidth monitoring, bedtime scheduling, and local DNS ad-blocking), the Application processes the following data strictly within your local area network (LAN):
- **Local Network Identifiers:** Local IP addresses, Media Access Control (MAC) addresses, and local device hostnames/manufacturer names.
- **Local Traffic Metrics:** Real-time throughput and bandwidth counters (upload/download speed) processed in real-time.
- **User Configurations:** Custom device nicknames, sleep timers, schedules, and user-defined blocking preferences.

#### Information We DO NOT Collect:
- We **DO NOT** collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- We **DO NOT** inspect, record, log, or track your personal browsing history, website contents, search queries, passwords, or encrypted payload data.
- We **DO NOT** collect location data, telemetry, or diagnostic tracking data.

---

### 2. DATA STORAGE AND TRANSFERS (100% OFFLINE & LOCAL)

- **Local Storage Only:** All device listings, custom tags, and scheduling rules are stored exclusively on your local machine in an offline SQLite database.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** SwitchGate Internet does not transmit, synchronize, or upload any of your network data or device configurations to any remote cloud servers, external databases, or third-party servers.
- **No Analytics / No Trackers:** The Application contains no third-party tracking SDKs, telemetry frameworks, or advertising libraries.

---

### 3. THIRD-PARTY SHARING

We **do not** sell, trade, rent, or share any user information or network data with any third parties under any circumstances.

---

### 4. SYSTEM PERMISSIONS & CAPABILITIES

SwitchGate Internet requests standard Windows desktop capabilities (including full-trust execution and local network access):
- **Local Network Access:** Required to perform local ARP discovery, resolve local hostnames, and measure local gateway traffic.
- **Administrative Privileges (Optional / When Enabled):** Required strictly for managing local firewall drop rules and executing raw-socket network controls locally on your own machine.

---

### 5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

SwitchGate Internet is a network utility tool that does not target, collect, or store any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

---

### 6. USER CONTROL & DATA RETENTION

You have complete control over your data:
- All stored local data remains on your computer as long as the application is installed.
- Uninstalling SwitchGate Internet or clearing its local application folder immediately and permanently purges all local databases and configuration files.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect feature enhancements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
